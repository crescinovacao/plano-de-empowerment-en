Empowerment Plan

A tool to help you map which tasks you can delegate, assess the ideal empowerment stage for each situation, and build an action plan to develop your team's autonomy.

How to use

  • A. List the tasks and activities of yours that can be delegated.
  • B. Indicate how much of your routine each task takes up.
  • C. Identify the person with the greatest potential to take on the task, considering availability, skill and motivation.
  • D. Rate that person's competence from 1 to 10 (1 = low, 10 = high).
  • E. Rate the activity's risk to the business from 1 to 10 (1 = low, 10 = high).
  • F. The tool automatically calculates the recommended empowerment stage.

Empowerment plan

The stage is calculated automatically from Competence minus Risk.

How to apply each stage

Use this guide as support to build your action plan, right below.

Action plan

Choose one of the activities and build a plan to take the chosen person through the next empowerment stages, until they reach the empowerment stage (Let Them Do It).
